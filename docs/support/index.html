Support

Write to hello.andyapp@gmail.com. A person reads every message.

Deleting things

A note: open it, tap Edit, then Delete this note. A person: on their page, tap Edit, then Delete at the bottom. Notes that mention them keep the name they were written with, and it stops opening anything. Everything: Settings → Delete account removes every person, note and photo, and your sign-in with it. This cannot be undone.

What Andy sends, and asking first

Reading a note, a photo's words, searching by meaning and drafting a follow-up are done by Anthropic (Claude) and OpenAI. Andy asks the first time you reach for one of them, and sends nothing until you allow it. If you choose Not now, you can still save notes as you wrote them, and find people by name or by a word. Change your answer in Settings → What Andy sends. The details are in the privacy policy.

No signal

Everyone and every note can be read offline, and a new note is kept on your iPhone until you are back online. Changes made offline are saved for good when you tap Sync.

Before a meeting

Connect your calendar from the card on home, or in Settings → Calendar. Names are matched on your iPhone and never leave it. Reminders show a name, never what you wrote. You can mute them or turn them off in Settings.

Locking Andy

If your iPhone has Face ID, Touch ID or a passcode, Andy asks for it each time it comes back to the front.

Signing in

Andy uses Sign in with Apple. Signing out removes everything Andy kept on this iPhone; signing back in brings it back from your account.

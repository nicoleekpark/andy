Option D - Pages

Every version is a page in a small deck; you see the edge of the neighbours and dots under them. Andy never writes over a page: asking makes a new page and turns to it. Going back is a swipe (or tap a dot or the edge). Closest to the rejected round's one field, so the test is whether pages, not a panel, make "go back" obvious. Copy only; nothing is sent.

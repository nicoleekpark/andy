Option E - Receipt

One page, laid out like a receipt: what it is at the top in label rows, the message itself as the big headline, one small line under it for what you tell Andy, and the versions as a history table at the bottom. No "write it myself / ask Andy" choice: leave the message empty and tell Andy what to write; or write it and ask Andy to tidy it; or select words and ask about just those. Copy only - nothing is sent. Light mode, tokens as in theme.ts; the selection tint is the iOS system selection.

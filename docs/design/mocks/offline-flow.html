The one idea

Andy has two kinds of work. Writing down what you typed or changed needs nothing, so Andy does it right away, on the phone. Understanding a new note (who is it about, what to remember) needs the AI, and the AI lives on the internet. So a new note waits for the internet, and a change waits for you to press Sync.

Good to know
  • Everything kept on the phone is private to Andy, behind Face ID, and never shown to another account.
  • If you Sign out with notes or changes still waiting, Andy warns you first, because signing out deletes them.
  • Asking in your own words ("who knows Marcus") and drafting a follow-up need the AI, so those say "needs a connection" offline. Searching by name or by a word (dog, lover) works offline.

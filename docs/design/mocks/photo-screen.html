Photo — the third door of the orbit

Design prototype, not production code. Opened from capture-orbit → Photo (2 o'clock), or from someone's page. Owner's answers of 2026-10-13: the camera opens at once with the album beside the shutter; the shutter goes straight to reading; the words step shows a small photo to compare against; one photo per note. Pick a step below. The shutter, album, thumbnail and Retake are live too.

1 · Camera, at once

Andy's own camera (expo-camera), full screen: ✕ top-left, a hint, the shutter in the middle and the album bottom-left, where the iPhone Camera keeps it. The camera is the one dark screen in Andy, because a viewfinder on paper would fight the picture.

The hint is the scope in plain words: "A card, a page of notes, or a screenshot."

First time and camera off

iOS asks for the camera once, over this screen, at the moment of use. If you say no, the viewfinder is replaced by a line and Choose a photo instead, so the album still works without the camera. The album uses iOS's photo picker and never asks for Photos access (#155).

2 · Reading

The shutter goes straight to "Reading the photo…" with the photo shown, with no Use / Retake step. Offline never gets here: the Photo circle already says "Needs a connection".

3 · Check the words

A small photo on top: tap to see it full size and compare a name. Under it, WHAT THE PHOTO SAYS ABOUT, then who: a name field the same call that reads the photo fills in ("Priya", or the card's "Sarah Chen"), and the words without that name line (owner, 2026-10-13). Both are editable, with one line: "Fix any name or word, then let Andy read it."

No name on the page: the field shows who? and the words stay whole. Opened from Priya's page: "Priya" is fixed, not a field, because the page already said who. Cards follow the same check as voice (R10), with no exception.

The saved note's words start after the name. Nothing is lost, because the note is filed under that person.

Let Andy read it → the usual review → Remember this. Retake (from the album: Choose another) throws this photo away and goes back.

No words

Nothing readable: "Andy couldn't find any words in this photo. Try again closer, with more light." and Retake. Nothing is saved.

What is kept

The words, not the photo. The photo is used to read and then thrown away (the phone's copy goes with the screen, and with sign-out). Keeping photos would be a new promise to make and to pay for; not in V1.

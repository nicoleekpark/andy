Write to Priya

HTML approximation of native iOS screens — a design prototype for discussion, not production code. Example people and messages are fictional fixtures (Priya, 수진), per follow-up-redesign.md (owner decisions, 2026-10-21) and follow-up-reference-patterns.md.

Visual pass, same one flow: Door → Reply (paste or screenshot) or Reach out → shared refine. Built deliberately from ten patterns in Grammarly, Gmail, Apple Writing Tools, Superhuman and Outlook — their message leads, intents sit right under it, tone is three one-tap pills, one draft stays in view and steps through versions in place, and Copy is the one plainly prominent action. No AI sparkle, nothing that reads as Send.

Option A - Point and ask

One page. Type into it. To change a part, select it: a bar appears above the keyboard offering to have Andy change just that. With nothing selected, Andy works on the whole text. Copy only; nothing is sent. Light mode, tokens as in theme.ts; the pale selection tint is the iOS system selection, not an app colour.

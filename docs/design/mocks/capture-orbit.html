What this flow is

Home's round button (capture-orbit-trigger, new) keeps its slot, size and shape from home-actions.md's shipped V-NOTCHED Record — but it no longer records anything itself. It opens capture-orbit (new): a scrim over the whole screen, Google-Calendar-style, with three equal, round doors fanning out — Voice at 12, Write at 10, Photo at 2 o'clock — and the trigger itself becomes a close (✕). Tapping the scrim, the ✕, or any door closes it.

1–2 — the trigger and the orbit

Two trigger labels, switchable above: T1 draws "+" inside the moss circle with "New note" set beneath it as its own caption (capture-orbit-trigger-caption) — recognition over recall, the exact shape record-button already had, with its word moved outside the circle rather than inside it. T2 is "+" alone; VoiceOver still says "New note" on either. Both converge to a bare ✕ while open — no caption needed there, the same established icon-only exception settings-button's gearshape already uses (one universally recognised symbol, nothing competing for its meaning).

Each orbit item is a 56pt moss circle (icon) with its word in a solid paper chip directly beneath — never bare ink text floating on the scrim itself. This matters for contrast: the scrim is ink at 42% composited over whatever is behind it, which measures as a mid grey, not a reliable 4.5:1 backdrop for text at full opacity. Every other dialog in this app already follows this rule — confirm-dialog's text sits on a solid paper card floating above the scrim, never directly on it — this is the same rule, applied to a smaller shape.

Geometry (toggle "geometry overlay" above): each item sits at radius 120pt from the trigger's centre — Write and Photo at ±104pt horizontal / -60pt vertical (60° off vertical), Voice at 0 / -120pt (straight up). At 390pt width with the trigger centred, the widest item's outer edge lands at ~328pt from the right edge, ~62pt from the left — both comfortably inside the 16pt margin either side. Approximate, confirm on device, the same caveat this project's other geometry mocks already carry. Because the orbit is bilaterally symmetric around a centred trigger, it is identical for a right- or left-handed grip — no handedness trade-off, the same finding capture-radial.html's arc already established.
This supersedes, for this flow: home-actions.md's own copy for record-button ("keeps the visible word 'Record'") — the round button's word is now "New note" (reusing capture-doors.md's own C6), and its role is a chooser, not a recorder. record-flow.md's R2 ("recording starts only on an explicit tap of mic-button") is amended: tapping the Voice door is that explicit tap, so record--listening opening already-listening does not violate R2's intent, it relocates where the explicit tap happens.

Copy flagged for product-strategist

3 — Voice opens already listening

Tapping Voice (toggle "voice: listening" above) opens record--listening exactly as record-flow.md's Final choice already drew it: the red (alert) "Recording" indicator with a live m:ss timer, the growing transcript, a large Done and a small Pause beside it — carried here unchanged, including Pause's own "Paused · m:ss" + Resume + Done (toggle "voice: paused"). On the very first ever use (toggle "voice: first ever use"), the system microphone prompt appears before any audio is captured — the same escape hatch capture-doors.md's M3 proposed for exactly this problem — and only once it is granted does listening begin; declining returns to home with the typing door still fully open, per capture-doors.md R4/R5.

4 — Write opens with the keyboard already up

From home (toggle "write: from home"), about-field is empty with the cursor already in it (shown here with a blinking caret, frozen under Reduce Motion) and the keyboard is already up — no second tap to focus the field. The body shows the system ClipboardPasteButton when empty, per capture-doors.md's "answered once" §R7. From a person's page (toggle "write: from Jordan Lee's page"), about-field is already declared — the same about-field--declared shape record-flow.md's Option A settled, with a Change link — and the cursor moves to the body instead, since the subject is already known.

5 — Photo opens the camera directly

card-scan (new): a full-screen camera on expo-camera, no VisionKit, no live edge tracking — the frame guide (card-scan-frame, four static corner brackets) never moves or reacts to what's under it, which is the honest reading of "no live edge detection." card-scan-close-button top-left, card-scan-album-button bottom-left (opens the system photo picker directly — no Photos permission prompt, capture-doors.md R13), card-scan-shutter-button large and centred. No mode tabs: Andy reads a card and a handwritten page the same way. After capture (toggle "photo: captured"), the photo shows with card-scan-retake-button (plain text) and card-scan-use-photo-button (primary pill) — then record--picture (toggle "photo: words check"), already named in capture-doors.md: the lead and label swap to "the card" / "What the card said" under scope S-b, where every photo is a card, and stay generic ("the picture" / "What the picture said", C9/C10) under S-a. One photo per note (capture-doors.md R14) — there is no "add another" control anywhere in this screen.

Supersedes capture-doors.md's own "answered once" §R9/call 4 (one Photo item opening a Take/Choose sheet): this flow's Photo door opens the camera immediately, with the album as an in-screen button rather than a separate step. One fewer tap to the camera; the cost is that someone who only ever wants the library now sees the camera UI first, even briefly, before reaching for the album button — named rather than hidden, since it is a real, if small, change of direction from that earlier decision.

(this designer's extrapolation of C13 to cover notes, not yet in the copy table — flag). S-b: "Andy needs the camera to take a photo of a card. You can turn it on in Settings." (C13, verbatim).

6 — Offline, said before anything opens

Toggle "connection: offline". While the orbit is open, the Photo item's own label swaps inline from "Photo"/"Card" to a second, quiet line reading "Needs a connection" (ink at textOpacity.quiet, 5.0:1 on the item's paper chip — an approved pairing, not a new one) — visible before the tap, not only after. Tapping it anyway (toggle "photo: offline") shows photo-offline-notice as a confirm-dialog-shaped card over the still-open orbit, never opening the camera first and failing. Voice and Write are completely unaffected offline, exactly as today.

Wording discrepancy, named rather than resolved: this task's own offline sentence — "Photos need a connection to be read. Take it with the iPhone Camera now and choose it here later." — differs slightly from capture-doors.md's already-decided C11 ("Reading a photo needs a connection. Take it with your Camera now and choose it here later, or write it instead."). This mock uses the newer wording as given for this task; flag for product-strategist to pick one and update the other document, so the app doesn't end up with two true sentences for the same state.

7 — Scope switch (S-a / S-b)

Toggle "scope" above to compare, side by side, the two answers to capture-doors.md's own open scope question (§12.1): S-a "Notes and cards" — the frame hint says "Fit the note or card inside the frame," the door says "Photo," and record--picture stays generic, because its free-text editor already copes with several names in one note. S-b "Cards only" — the hint says "Fit the card inside the frame," the door says "Card," and record--picture's own lead/label specialise to "the card" / "What the card said," matching copy-build-4's existing precedent for a card's busy state ("Reading the card…", C14). Neither changes card-scan's own mechanics — the camera, the frame, the shutter and the words-check step are identical either way; only three words move.

All four doors are reached in exactly 2 taps from home at rest — the orbit's own cost (one extra tap to open it, same as capture-doors.md's M1) is paid once, equally, by every door, rather than by Voice alone. This is the trade this flow makes deliberately: Voice is no longer 1 tap (as M2/M3/R2/R3 achieved elsewhere in this project's exploration), in exchange for every door — including the two rarer ones — being exactly as fast to reach as Voice is, and none of them living behind a smaller or secondary control.

Accessibility

  • Modal, explicit order. capture-orbit sets accessibilityViewIsModal; on open, focus moves to Voice first, then Write, then Photo, then the trigger-as-close last — Voice → Write → Photo → Close, per the brief. Toggle "voiceover order" above for the numbered badges live on the screen. The two-finger-Z escape gesture or a swipe past Photo also closes it.
  • Targets. Each orbit item's badge is 56pt; its full hit area (badge + label chip) exceeds 44×44pt. The trigger is 72pt. card-scan-shutter-button is 72pt, card-scan-album-button and -close-button are 44×44pt.
  • No icon-only doors. Every orbit item shows its word in the paper chip beneath its icon. The camera's shutter, close and album controls are icon-only, justified the same way settings-button's gearshape already is — each is a single, universally recognised camera convention (Camera, Instagram, Snapchat) with a VoiceOver label, not a command hidden with no spoken equivalent.
  • Reduce Motion (toggle above): the fan-out and the trigger's icon swap drop to a 120ms opacity crossfade only, no translate or scale; the recording dot and the field caret both freeze rather than pulse/blink.
  • Dynamic Type (toggle above): at the largest accessibility size, the orbit hides and converges to orbit-type-fallback, a plain full-width stacked list — Voice, Write, Photo in that order — the same convergence pattern capture-radial.html's arc already established, so there is no new fallback shape to design or test.
  • Contrast, no new pairings. Orbit item words: ink on paper chip (12.0:1). Offline meta: ink at textOpacity.quiet on the same chip (5.0:1). card-scan's hint text is paper on a dark camera placeholder — not a themed pairing (the backdrop is sensor content, not an app surface), kept at full opacity precisely so it stays legible regardless of what the lens sees.
  • Colour never carries meaning alone. Recording says "Recording" and the time, not just red; offline says "Needs a connection" and the explanation dialog's own sentence, not just a dimmed icon; denied states always pair with a sentence and a way forward (Settings, or the album).

Names proposed, if this ships

  • capture-orbit-trigger (new) — the round button on home; states --open (✕). Takes over record-button's slot and size; no longer records.
  • capture-orbit (new) — the open radial menu: the scrim, the three items, the trigger acting as Close.
  • capture-orbit-item--voice / --write / --photo (named in the brief) — the three round doors.
  • card-scan (new) — the full-screen camera. card-scan-frame, -shutter-button, -album-button (all named in the brief), -close-button, -retake-button, -use-photo-button, -denied-line, -open-settings-button (added here, following the brief's own naming shape).
  • record--picture and photo-offline-notice — already proposed in capture-doors.md, reused unchanged; here photo-offline-notice is triggered directly from the orbit's Photo item rather than from a photo-source sheet, since this flow has no sheet to trigger it from.

No new tokens proposed — every surface reuses moss, ink, paper, edge, line, placeholder, scrim, and the one named alert exception (the recording indicator).

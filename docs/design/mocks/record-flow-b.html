What this option is

record--ready becomes one large circular mic-button--primary (new) filling the lower, thumb-reachable half of the screen — the shape Camera and Voice Memos already teach (Jakob's law), in moss rather than the red those apps use, because STYLE.md keeps alert for errors and three deletions. scan-card-button and type-instead-button drop to a slim text row beneath it. This is the most literal read of §6's "mic-button is the dominant control".

about-field, typing only

On typing, about-field starts collapsed as one quiet line, "+ Add who this is about" — tap it to expand into the field and suggestions (progressive disclosure). record--transcript carries no about-field at all: voice relies on the spoken name and review's existing check, exactly as today (brief R9, option b).

VoiceOver, collapsed row: "Add who this is about, button"

Declared from a page (R7)

From Jordan Lee's page, typing shows one sentence — "This note goes to Jordan Lee." — with a Change link beside it, never a field box: reads most clearly as "already decided", since there is nothing that looks editable to tap by accident.

Accessibility

  • mic-button--primary is 148×148pt, well over the 44pt floor; its label is inside the circle, which must be tested at the largest Dynamic Type sizes to confirm "Record"/"Stop"/"Starting…" never clips inside a fixed circle — a risk this option owns and Option A/C do not.
  • The collapsed chip's tap target is a full 44pt row even though its label is short, per brief §8.
  • Listening changes by label and live words only, never colour.

Risk flagged against the brief

R9 recommends option (a) ("no name is pre-filled by guessing... a place to fix a misheard name before anything is read"). This option is the brief-sanctioned way to show (b) for comparison, not a disagreement with the recommendation.

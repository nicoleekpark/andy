Revision — 2026-10-13

Four changes on top of the redesign below, from the owner's second pass: (1) the transcript now starts at the top of its area and grows downward — try the "Long" transcript toggle on Listening to see it fill and auto-scroll, rather than sitting pinned to the bottom from the first word. (2) The word "Recording" and its pulsing dot are gone; the listening state is now carried by the running timer alone, the centre control's red pause glyph, and a one-time VoiceOver announcement when listening starts (the small banner that flashes near the top when you tap the mic stands in for it — this HTML cannot speak). (3) The three controls are remapped: LEFT is always a quiet trash icon (Start over, no longer hidden until words exist), CENTRE is the red pause/resume toggle, RIGHT is a filled moss checkmark (Done) — pausing no longer sits where finishing used to, and finishing no longer sits in the centre. (4) Each of the three now carries a small visible text label beneath it ("Start over" / "Pause" or "Resume" / "Done"), matching its VoiceOver name exactly. The sections below are updated in place to describe the current shape, not kept as two histories to reconcile by hand.

What changed here

The waveform becomes the live transcript — now the largest thing on screen, not a decoration beside it. The timer keeps the reference's exact position: centred, below the transcript, above the controls. The bottom row keeps the reference's three-control shape, each labelled — rather than today's full-width text pill plus a quiet "Start over" link underneath it.

Adapting the reference, deliberately

The reference's card sits on a visibly different, lighter page. Andy has one background tone (paper) everywhere — STYLE.md's whole palette is six colours, and adding a second neutral or a shadow token for one screen is exactly the ad-hoc drift the tokens rule exists to prevent. The "card" here is a 1px colors.edge outline at radius.card, inset by space.sm from the screen edges — it reads as a card through its shape alone, the same discipline that already dropped the Briefing card's torn-edge border (STYLE.md, Signature Element). No token proposed.

The "before" state is rare now

Under the orbit flow, tapping Voice starts listening at once — record--ready (mic glyph, MAIN alone) now only appears on the very first permission ask and after Start over, not on every open. The old secondary row — "Scan a business card" / "Type it instead" — is gone: Write and Photo are now their own orbit doors, not links on this screen (capture-doors.md, "Owner's orbit flow").

Flagged for product-strategist: the decided mic-denied line ends "…or type it instead," which assumed the old inline link. There is no door to Write on this screen any more — only the back chevron. Recommend either routing that phrase to "go back and write it instead" or confirming the back chevron is enough.

Start over is always reachable; emptiness decides whether it asks

This replaces the previous rule ("Start over, once words exist"), where LEFT was reserved but invisible until the first word. The owner's remap gives LEFT a fixed, always-visible role (trash, Start over), so hiding it would now mean MAIN and RIGHT shift sideways between states — the exact jump the old rule existed to prevent, just moved to a different control. Instead, the confirmation still depends on whether there is anything to lose: tapping it with no words yet goes straight back to record--ready, no dialog, the same "empty leaves without asking" rule record-flow.md's R12 already applies to leaving record--transcript. Once there are heard words, it opens start-over-alert as before. Try "Not started" vs. "Short" on the transcript toggle, then tap the trash icon on Listening, to feel the difference.

The CENTRE ring's live indication — now on Pause, not Done

Per the brief: "not a fake progress arc." The ring around CENTRE tints to alert and pulses slowly while actually listening — a uniform tint, never a sweep or a fill that could be read as measuring something — and now holds the pause glyph rather than the old stop glyph, since finishing moved to RIGHT. It reverts to the quiet edge ring and a plain ink glyph, static, the moment it pauses (the glyph itself also changes shape, pause → play, so the state is never carried by colour alone even for someone who can't see red as red). Reduce Motion keeps the tint, drops the pulse — colour and shape still carry the state; motion never has to.

Done moves to RIGHT, and becomes the one filled control

The previous version filled MAIN with moss for the finishing action. The owner's remap frees the centre for the red pause/resume toggle instead, so Done — a 56pt checkmark circle — is now the only moss-filled control on this screen, deliberately on the opposite end from the trash icon: destructive and finishing stay as far apart as a three-slot row allows, and the fill alone (smaller than CENTRE, but the one spot of colour among three outlines) is enough to mark it as "the one that moves you on," the same Von Restorff logic this document already uses for mic-button on record--ready.

After Done: no new screen

The card's content swaps in place for exactly record-flow.md Option A's already-decided record--transcript shape — transcript-lead, the unchanged about-field, the editable note-source-field, read-button / record-again-button — reflowed inside this card, not a push to another screen. The three round controls are gone entirely here, which is the "reduces sensibly" the brief asked for: once there is nothing left to pause or discard mid-sentence, a pill and a plain link say more plainly what to do next than three icons would.

VoiceOver

Order: transcript → timer/status → Start over → Pause/Resume → Done, the task's own left-to-right order on screen, so no override is needed. CENTRE: "Pause" / "Resume" — no longer "Done talking." RIGHT: "Done" — no longer "Pause." Both now have a visible on-screen label too (below the icon), so for the first time on this screen the spoken name and the visible word are identical (2.5.3 satisfied directly, not by exemption). LEFT: "Start over," unchanged in name, now always present rather than appearing only once words exist — a VoiceOver user no longer has to discover it by swiping into an element that wasn't there a moment ago. record-timer gets its own accessibilityLabel in words ("1 minute 42 seconds elapsed"), not the raw "1:42" string — VoiceOver reads digits-with-colon oddly. Not a live region: read on request, per record-flow.md's Final choice, not announced every second. The caret is decorative and hidden from VoiceOver. Listening no longer has a status word on screen at all (the dot and "Recording" are both gone) — instead, AccessibilityInfo.announceForAccessibility("Recording.") fires once, when listening starts, which this mock shows as a visible banner since it cannot speak. Paused keeps its "Paused" word, read in the normal reading order.

Accessibility

  • Targets: CENTRE 76pt, LEFT/RIGHT 56pt (up from 52pt), both over the 44pt floor.
  • Contrast: edge ring 3.2:1 (WCAG 1.4.11); alert ring/glyph ≥3:1 for a meaningful graphic (the already-tested pairing record-flow.md's Final choice approved for the recording indicator); moss/paper on the Done button reuses the pairing already approved for read-button and name-choice--selected — no new pairing; ink on paper 12:1 for every icon, label and the transcript; dimmed transcript lines and the paused label never below textOpacity.quiet (5.0:1).
  • No colour-only state: listening vs. paused differs in three non-colour ways at once — the timer runs or sits frozen, the centre glyph's shape changes (pause bars → play triangle, not just colour), and "Paused" is a word on screen. Colour (the red ring) is one more cue layered on top, never the only one.
  • Dynamic Type: the transcript and the three control labels grow; the three round controls keep their fixed size (the labels wrap under them rather than stretching the circle) and stay reachable — verify on a device, which this HTML cannot simulate.
  • Reduce Motion: the CENTRE ring's tint-pulse, the transcript caret and the VoiceOver-announcement banner's fade all drop their animation and hold a static, still-visible (or instantly-hidden) state — try the Motion toggle above.
  • Permission-denied and no-on-device-speech states are both in the "ready error" rig control; the typing door this error used to offer is flagged above, not silently kept.

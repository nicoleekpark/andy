Andy's promises

  1. Only you. Your notes are never shared, sold or shown to anyone.
  2. Andy never reaches out. It doesn't message anyone. Drafts stay with you.
  3. Quiet on the lock screen. Reminders show a name, never what you wrote.
  4. No ads, no tracking. Not now, not later.
  5. Gone when you say. Delete a note, a person, or everything, in the app.

The short version

  • Andy is a private notebook for remembering what the people you meet tell you. Your notes are yours, and they are about people who have not signed up for Andy, so we treat them with the same care you would.
  • To organise and search your notes, their text is sent to our service providers, including Anthropic (Claude) and OpenAI. Nothing is sold, and there are no ads or tracking.
  • Voice is turned into text on your iPhone when it can be. Andy never saves audio recordings.
  • Calendar access is optional. If you allow it, Andy reads your upcoming meetings to match them to people you keep, and never changes your calendar.
  • You can delete a note, a person, or your whole account from inside the app at any time.

What Andy collects, and why

Your account

You sign in with Apple. We receive the name and email address Apple shares with us, which may be a private relay address if you choose to hide your email. We use them to identify your account and keep your notes separate from everyone else's.

Your notes

When you record or type a note, Andy stores its text and what it pulls out of it: the names of the people or animals it is about, how you know them, when you first met, facts you want to remember, tags, and short quotes about anyone else the note mentions. You can review and change all of this before a note is saved, and edit it afterwards.

Your voice

Speech is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition. Andy asks for this to happen on your iPhone. If your iPhone cannot recognise speech on the device for the language in use, Apple's servers do it instead, under Apple's privacy policy. Andy keeps the text, never the audio, and only listens after you tap Record.

Photos

Profile photos you add to a person are stored with your account and shown only to you. Business card photos are sent to Anthropic so the card's text can be read. The photo itself is not stored; the text read from it becomes the note, which you can check before saving.

Your calendar (optional)

If you allow calendar access, Andy reads the title, time and attendee names of meetings in the next twelve hours and sends them to our servers to see whether any of them name someone you keep. They are used for that match and not stored. Andy never adds, changes or deletes anything in your calendar. Calendar details are not sent to any AI provider.

Notifications (optional)

If you allow notifications, Andy schedules reminders on your iPhone before and after matched meetings. The reminder shows the person's name and the meeting title, never anything you wrote about them.

App lock

Andy can be locked with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. iOS handles this entirely; Andy never receives your face, fingerprint or passcode.

About the people in your notes

Andy exists to help you remember what people chose to tell you, so you can ask about it next time. The people you write about have not agreed to be in Andy, and they cannot see what you write. A good rule: keep notes you would be comfortable with them reading, and leave out sensitive details you don't need. Andy never contacts anyone: follow-up drafts are shown only to you, and nothing is sent unless you copy it and send it yourself.

If you believe someone keeps notes about you in Andy, write to [contact email]. Notes belong to the account that wrote them and we cannot read them to you, but we will explain how Andy works and handle any request the law gives you.

What Andy does not do

  • No advertising, and no selling or renting of your data.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • No reading of your messages, email or contacts.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and stored with providers that encrypt it at rest. It is not end-to-end encrypted: Andy's AI features have to read the text of a note to organise and search it. Access to your account needs your Apple sign-in, and the app can be locked on your iPhone.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept until you delete it. You can delete a single note or a whole person at any time. Settings → Delete account deletes every person, note and photo in your account, and your sign-in with it. This cannot be undone. Copies held by service providers are removed under their own retention policies.

Children

Andy is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
